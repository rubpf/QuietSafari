QuietSafari

For Safari on Mac. Built in Xcode with a free Apple ID.

A quieter
Safari.

Hides banners, trackers, YouTube ads, and Yandex ads on other sites. Yandex search and maps stay put.

Download Xcode required GitHub

macOS 14 or later. The download is the Xcode project, not an app.

What goes quiet

YouTubePre-rolls are stripped from the player response before they start.
BannersEasyList hides ordinary display ads and the empty slots they leave.
TrackersEasyPrivacy, including Yandex Metrica.
YandexYandex and Adfox ads on other sites. Not search, and not maps.

Three steps

1
Install Xcode and open the project

Install Xcode from the App Store. Unzip the download and open QuietSafari.xcodeproj.

2
Sign with your Apple ID and Run

Set your Personal Team (a free Apple ID) on all 5 targets, then press Run.

3
Turn the extensions on

Safari → Settings → Advanced → Show features for web developers. Develop → Allow Unsigned Extensions. Tick the 4 extensions: QuietSafari, Content Blocker, Privacy, and Russian. That toggle turns off every time Safari quits; turn it on again.

FAQ

Why do the checkboxes reset?

A free Apple ID build is unsigned as far as Safari is concerned. Safari turns off Allow Unsigned Extensions when it quits. Turn that on again, then tick the four extensions. QuietSafari says the permission was reset and offers a button back to the settings.

Does anything leave this Mac?

No. Rules and settings stay on this computer. Update filters only downloads the public EasyList, EasyPrivacy, and RU AdList files.

Which macOS?

macOS 14 or later. This build does not cover iPhone.
